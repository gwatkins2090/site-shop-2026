## IMAGE SUBMISSION GUIDELINES

### Required Images:
1. **Artist Portrait Photo**
   - Format: JPG or PNG
   - Minimum size: 800x800 pixels (square format preferred)
   - High quality, good lighting

2. **Logo/Icon (optional)**
   - Format: JPG, PNG, or SVG
   - Size: 100x100 pixels minimum
   - Simple design that works at small sizes

3. **Artwork Images**
   - Format: JPG or PNG
   - Minimum size: 1200x1200 pixels
   - High resolution for best quality
   - Multiple angles/views optional but recommended

### How to Submit Images:
Please organize your images in folders and send via:
- Cloud storage link (Google Drive, Dropbox, etc.)
- Email (if files are small enough)
- USB drive (if meeting in person)
- WeTransfer or similar file transfer service

**Folder structure example:**
```
YourName_Portfolio_Images/
  ├── artist-portrait.jpg
  ├── logo.png
  └── artworks/
      ├── artwork-1-ethereal-landscapes.jpg
      ├── artwork-2-urban-reflections.jpg
      ├── artwork-3-abstract-emotions.jpg
      └── ...
```
