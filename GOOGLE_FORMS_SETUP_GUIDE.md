# Google Forms Setup Guide - Artist Portfolio Content Collection

This guide will help you create a Google Form in **5-10 minutes** using copy/paste. Your artists will love how easy it is to fill out!

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Go to **https://forms.google.com**
2. Click **"+ Blank"** to create a new form
3. Follow the instructions below - just copy/paste each question!

---

## ⚙️ Form Settings (Do This First!)

1. Click the **Settings (gear icon)** at the top right
2. Under **"General"** tab:
   - ✅ Check **"Limit to 1 response"** (optional)
   - ✅ Check **"Allow response editing"** (lets them save and come back!)
3. Under **"Presentation"** tab:
   - ✅ Check **"Show progress bar"**
   - ✅ Check **"Shuffle question order"** - UNCHECK this (keep order)
4. Click **"Save"**

---

## 🎨 Final Form Setup

### Confirmation Message
After adding all questions, set up the confirmation message:

1. Click **Settings (gear icon)** → **Presentation** tab
2. Scroll to **"Confirmation message"**
3. Replace the default text with:

```
Thank you! 🎉

Your content has been submitted successfully. We'll review your information and reach out within 24-48 hours with next steps.

In the meantime, if you think of anything you'd like to add or change, you can edit your responses using the link sent to your email.

Questions? Reply to our email or reach out anytime!
```

---

## 📧 Email Collection

To automatically email responses to artists:

1. Click the **Responses** tab at the top
2. Click the three dots **⋮**
3. Select **"Get email notifications for new responses"**
4. Check **"Collect email addresses"** if you want their email automatically

---

## 🎁 Form Theme/Appearance

Make it look nice:

1. Click the **palette icon** at the top
2. Choose a color theme that matches your brand
3. Select a header image (optional - could use an artwork image)
4. Pick a background color

---

## ✅ You're Done!

**Your form is ready!** Here's what to do next:

1. Click **"Send"** button at top right
2. Copy the form link
3. Send it to your artist with a friendly message like:

```
Hi [Artist Name]!

Here's the form to gather all the content for your portfolio website:
[PASTE FORM LINK]

It takes about 30-45 minutes to complete, and your progress auto-saves so you can come back to it anytime.

Let me know if you have any questions!
```

---

## 📊 Viewing Responses

When artists submit:

1. Go to your form
2. Click the **"Responses"** tab
3. View in **"Summary"** (charts) or **"Individual"** (per person)
4. Click **"Create Spreadsheet"** icon to export to Google Sheets

All uploaded files will be linked in the spreadsheet!

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

- **Preview your form:** Click the eye icon 👁️ to test it before sending
- **Make a copy:** File → Make a copy (for reuse with future clients)
- **Send reminders:** Check who hasn't completed it in the Responses tab
- **For multiple artworks:** After they submit, send them the form link again just for the artwork section, or create a separate "Add More Artworks" form
